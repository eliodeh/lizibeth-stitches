Our Story

Itua Stitches was born from a deep passion for fashion and a desire to celebrate the African woman. Founded by Ejembe Itua, we have grown from a small shop in gwrinpa Abuja into a brand that women trust for elegance, quality and style.

Every piece we create tells a story. From bridal collections to casual wear, evening gowns to accessories, we pour our hearts into every stitch.
